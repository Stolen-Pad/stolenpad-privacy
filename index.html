Privacy Policy – Liquid Balance

Developer: StolenPad Studio · Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Liquid Balance (the "App"), published on Google Play by StolenPad Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

Liquid Balance does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. You can play without an account, and the App does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your game, the App stores your progress on your own device only: levels completed, stars, in-game coins and gems, event progress and settings such as sound and music. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your Android settings.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The App contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service and no social login, and it does not request permissions to access your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

4. Third parties

We do not sell, trade or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The App is downloaded through Google Play, which is operated by Google and may collect information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App is built with the Unity engine; Unity's analytics, ads and diagnostics services are turned off in this App.

5. In-game currency and rewards

Coins, gems and rewards in the App are virtual items that exist only inside the game on your device. They have no real-world value.

6. Children's privacy

Liquid Balance is suitable for players of all ages. Because the App does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data security and retention

Since your game data is kept only on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. We keep no copy of it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete; you can remove all game data at any time by uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App adds features that collect data (for example ads or analytics), we will update this page before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
