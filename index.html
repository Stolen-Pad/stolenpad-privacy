Privacy Policy – Liquid Balance and Balance Jam

Developer: StolenPad Studio · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile games Liquid Balance and Balance Jam (each, the "App"), published on Google Play by StolenPad Studio ("we", "us"), collect and use information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

You can play without an account, and the App never asks for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. To measure how the game is played and how players find it, the App uses analytics and attribution services from GameAnalytics, Adjust and Meta (Facebook), through the TinySauce SDK of our publishing partner Voodoo. These services receive device identifiers and gameplay events, as described below. We do not sell your personal data. The App does not show ads.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved on your own device: levels completed, stars, in-game coins, gems and boosters, event progress and settings such as sound and music. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in your Android settings.

3. Information collected by the App

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, country (derived from the IP address), app version and install date.
  • Identifiers: your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and identifiers generated by the services below. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings.
  • Gameplay events: when you start and finish a level, whether you won, your score (stars), session start and length, and similar in-game events.
  • Install attribution: how you found and installed the App (for example which store page or campaign).

We use this information to understand how the game is played, improve its levels and difficulty, measure its performance and evaluate it with our publishing partner.

4. Third-party services

The information above is processed by these services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Voodoo (TinySauce SDK and Voodoo analytics), our publishing partner: https://www.voodoo.io/privacy
  • GameAnalytics (game analytics): https://gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Adjust (install attribution and measurement): https://www.adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy/
  • Meta / Facebook (Facebook SDK app events, to measure installs from Facebook and Instagram): https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/
  • Google Play, through which the App is downloaded: https://policies.google.com/privacy

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with anyone else, except where required by law.

5. Your choices and consent

When required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App asks for your consent before analytics and attribution data is collected, and you can change your choice later in the App's privacy settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Data deletion

To ask us to delete data linked to your device, email stolenpad.studio@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – Liquid Balance" or "Data deletion – Balance Jam". We will forward the request to Voodoo, GameAnalytics, Adjust and Meta and confirm within 30 days. Uninstalling the App deletes all game data stored on your device.

7. In-game currency and rewards

Coins, gems and rewards in the App are virtual items that exist only inside the game. They have no real-world value.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the App and data about them was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data security and retention

Data sent to the services above is transmitted over encrypted connections and kept only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, in line with each provider's retention rules.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, contact us at the email below.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
